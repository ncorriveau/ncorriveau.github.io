About 😀

I am a self taught software engineer, currently working at Meta Reality Labs AI. I am passionate about learning new subjects with a particular focus on applied math, computing, machine learning & AI. My focus over the past couple of years has been on machine learning and deep learning and data efficient software systems. As part of my learning, I love to create guides that help me (and hopefully others) understand what can sometimes be considered opaque and difficult subjects. I plan on using this page to showcase some of that work, as well as write about my journey into a software engineering through self study.

Growing up, I played soccer ⚽️ at a very high level culminating in four years of Varsity Men's Soccer at UVA, which included a trip to the NCAA Final Four in 2013 and a National Championship in 2014.
